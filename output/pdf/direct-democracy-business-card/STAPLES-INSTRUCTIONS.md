# Staples ordering instructions

Design: A, with corrected Chicago stars. Product options checked October 20, 2026. Check the selected store's availability, current price, and pickup promise when ordering.

## Files to upload

- Front: `front-print.pdf`
- Back: `back-print.pdf`
- If the uploader accepts a two-page file for both sides, use `direct-democracy-chicago-print.pdf`: page 1 is the front; page 2 is the back.

Use the PDF files for printing. `front.png` and `back.png` are trimmed previews without bleed. Do not upload `front-and-back-preview.png`, which shows both sides together on a presentation background.

All print PDFs have a 3.75 x 2.25 inch page, including 0.125 inch bleed on every side. The finished card is 3.5 x 2 inches. Text and QR are vector; fonts are embedded. There are no crop marks or decorative borders. The QR points to https://www.waldgrave.com/directdemocracy and has been decoded from previews at 300 and 600 dpi.

## Upload and proof

1. Set the options above, then select Upload design under Use your own design.
2. Upload `front-print.pdf` to the front side and `back-print.pdf` to the back side. If it instead accepts a combined PDF, upload `direct-democracy-chicago-print.pdf` and assign page 1 to front and page 2 to back. Do not assume it assigned both pages correctly.
3. Keep both sides upright and landscape. Do not mirror either side or manually rotate the back upside down.
4. The 3.75 x 2.25 inch artwork includes bleed. It must fill the editor's full bleed canvas, with its center 3.5 x 2 inch area inside the trim lines. Do not shrink the entire PDF into the 3.5 x 2 inch trim area or add margins. If there are separate bleed and trim guides, align the artwork's outer edges with the bleed guides.
5. Review both sides in the finished-card preview. The front has the title and election-information line. The back has Your city. Your say., the QR, the URL, and The Waldgrave credit.
6. Check that the front's blue bands reach the cut edges, with no added white outline. White areas that are part of the design should stay white. All text must be readable and inside the safe guides; nothing should be clipped.
7. Check the QR's white margin. Do not crop, decorate, or put text over it. The code must remain square.
8. If the editor presents a border, unexplained cropping, or a resolution warning, do not approve that proof. Give a print associate the original PDFs and the print note below.
9. Approve the proof only when both sides look correct. Recheck Front & back, Matte, stock, quantity, selected store, total price, and promised pickup date before paying.
10. Save the order confirmation and proof for future reorders. Wait for the ready-for-pickup notice before going to the store.

The online business-card editor handles page placement. A browser's Print dialog is not part of this ordering process; do not print the PDFs to a new PDF using Fit to page.

## Pickup and quality check

Staples currently advertises free same-day pickup for eligible orders placed before 12 pm. Its page says orders may not be ready until 30 minutes before closing; later orders are ready the next day. Use the selected store's actual checkout promise when ordering.

Staples says a print associate can print a sample of a submitted same-day order. For the first batch, ask whether they can show you a sample before running the full quantity. Paper and ink can make the blue look different from a backlit screen.

At pickup, inspect a few cards: upright front and back, matte finish, clean cuts, readable small text, and no unwanted white slivers at the blue edges. Scan a physical card with your phone and confirm it opens the project page.

## Note to give the print associate

Please print double-sided, full color, landscape business cards on Standard 14pt matte stock, with square corners. Finished size: 3.5 x 2 inches. Supplied artwork: 3.75 x 2.25 inches including 0.125 inch bleed on each side. Front and back are supplied as separate PDFs; the combined PDF also has front on page 1 and back on page 2. Print with bleed, trim to the specified finished size, and keep both sides upright. Do not shrink the bleed artwork into the finished size or add a border. Please check a sample and its QR code before running the full batch if possible.
