## I: Moving star

Use case: ads-marketing.
Create one finished FRONT design for a collectible landscape 3.5 x 2 inch business card, ratio 7:4, for "direct democracy: Chicago". It must feel like a kinetic art poster shrunk into a pocket-sized keepsake. Flat full-frame artwork only: no mockup, no outer margin, no tabletop, no shadow, no presentation label.
A single enormous RED CHICAGO SIX-POINTED STAR is the visual anchor, with precisely six elongated points and broad inner body, NOT five points, NOT an asterisk, NOT two overlapping triangles. It is turned into a dramatic flowing screenprinted gesture with fluid sky-blue ribbons curling behind it. The star remains clearly readable, bold and red, asymmetric composition slightly to the right. Overlapping flat ink shapes form surprising negative spaces. A rhythmic balance of white paper, sky blue #41B6E6, pale blue #B3DDF2, rich red #C8102E and tiny deep black typography. Strong movement, elegance and a sense of physical ink on paper. Sophisticated midcentury experimental concert-poster energy with contemporary restraint. No dozens of little decorations, no generic geometric web branding, no gradients or 3D effects, no stock patriotic symbols.
Keep the entire leftmost 40 percent visually quiet white space for title to be professionally typeset later. The star should have the exact proportions of the Chicago flag star, with inner radius about 0.366 times outer radius and six 30-degree tips. Preserve that crisp symmetric outline. Motion happens in the blue ribbons around it. NO TEXT, no letters, no QR, no watermark. High resolution.
